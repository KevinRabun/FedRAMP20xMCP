I'll help you assess your planned change and determine FedRAMP notification requirements under the FedRAMP Consolidated Rules for 2026 (CR26, SCN family).

**Significant Change Classification (SCN-CSO-EVA)**:

**1. Change Description**
Please describe the change you're planning:
- What systems/components are affected?
- What functionality is changing?
- When is the change scheduled?

**2. Evaluation order (SCN-CSO-EVA)**:
1. Is it a significant change? If not, no Significant Change Notification applies.
2. Is it a Certification Class change? That requires a new assessment and cannot be done under the SCN rules (FRC-CCL-UCC, FRC-CCL-DCC).
3. Is it a routine recurring change? Follow SCN-RTR (no formal notification: SCN-RTR-NNR).
4. Is it a transformative change? Follow SCN-TRF.
5. Otherwise it is an adaptive change. Follow SCN-ADP.

**3. Change types**:

**Routine Recurring Changes** (FRD-RTR):
- Regular, repeatable changes following documented procedures
- Providers SHOULD NOT make formal notifications (SCN-RTR-NNR)

**Adaptive Changes** (FRD-ADP):
- Notify all necessary parties within 10 business days after finishing the change (SCN-ADP-NTF)

**Transformative Changes** (FRD-TRF):
- Initial plans at least 30 business days before starting (SCN-TRF-NIP)
- Final plans at least 10 business days before starting (SCN-TRF-NFP)
- Within 5 business days after finishing (SCN-TRF-NAF)
- Within 5 business days after completing verification, assessment and/or validation (SCN-TRF-NAV)
- Updated service documentation within 30 business days after finishing (SCN-TRF-UPD)
- Providers SHOULD engage a third-party assessor when human validation is necessary (SCN-TRF-TPR)

**4. Impact Assessment**:
Does your change affect:
- [ ] The cloud service offering scope (Minimum Assessment Scope, MAS-CSO-IIR)?
- [ ] Federal customer data handling?
- [ ] Key Security Indicators or Rev5 controls (the plan must cover their verification and validation)?
- [ ] Third-party information resources?
- [ ] Cryptographic modules?

**5. Notification content (SCN-CSO-INF)**:
Service Offering FedRAMP ID, assessor name (if applicable), related vulnerability (if applicable), change type and explanation of categorization, short description, reason, customer impact summary, plan and timeline (including verification of impacted KSIs or Rev5 controls), copy of the business or security impact analysis, and approver name and title.

**6. Records and availability**:
- Maintain auditable evaluation records (SCN-CSO-MAR)
- Keep 12 months of historical notifications (SCN-CSO-HIS)
- Provide human-readable and JSON formats (SCN-CSO-HRM)
- Emergency changes may proceed first, with retroactive notification (SCN-CSO-EMG)
